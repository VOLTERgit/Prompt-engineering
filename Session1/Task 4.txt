Selected App Feature: Instagram Explore Page
Why I selected this feature
I selected the Instagram Explore page because it shows content based on users' interests and activities.
AI can be used to generate content ideas that are suitable for a specific audience and social-media format.
Prompt for Instagram Explore Content
Detailed Prompt:
"Act as a social media content strategist who creates Instagram content ideas for the Explore page. Generate 5 Instagram Reel ideas for a small digital marketing and video editing business targeting Indian small-business owners aged 20–40.
For each Reel idea, provide:
1. A short and attention-grabbing hook for the first 3 seconds.
2. The main topic of the Reel.
3. A short 20–30 second script.
4. Suggested visuals or shots.
5. On-screen text.
6. A caption.
7. 5 relevant hashtags.
8. A simple call-to-action.
Keep the ideas practical and easy to create using a smartphone. Avoid clickbait and exaggerated claims. Use simple, natural English that an Indian audience can easily understand. Make each idea different from the others. Format the answer in a table."

| Reel | Topic | Hook | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 Social Media Mistakes | "Your business page may be losing customers because of these 3 mistakes." | "Save this Reel for later." |
| 2 | Better Instagram Reels | "Want better Reels without expensive equipment?" | "Follow for more tips." |
| 3 | Video Editing Tip | "This simple editing trick can make your videos look cleaner." | "Try this on your next Reel." |
| 4 | Google Ads Mistake | "Spending money on Google Ads but getting no leads?" | "Save this before your next campaign." |
| 5 | Content Ideas | "Don't know what to post today? Try these 5 ideas." | "Comment 'IDEAS' for more." |

Three or More "DOs" Used in This Prompt
The prompt follows several good prompting practices.
DO 1 – Give a clear role
I started the prompt with:
"Act as a social media content strategist..."

This gives the AI a clear role and helps it understand the type of response expected.
DO 2 – Give specific context
I specified:
"targeting Indian small-business owners aged 20–40."

This helps the AI create content for a particular audience instead of generating generic Instagram ideas.
DO 3 – Clearly explain the required output
I specifically requested:
- Hook
- Topic
- Script
- Visuals
- On-screen text
- Caption
- Hashtags
- CTA
This prevents the AI from giving only basic topic ideas.
DO 4 – Specify the format
I asked:
"Format the answer in a table."

This makes the output easier to read and compare.
DO 5 – Set limitations and style
I included:
"Use simple, natural English that an Indian audience can easily understand."

I also asked the AI to avoid clickbait and exaggerated claims.
This gives the AI a clear writing style and quality standard.

Overall Conclusion
Through this assignment, I learned that prompt engineering is not just about asking a question to an AI tool. It is about giving clear instructions so that the AI understands exactly what we need.
A good prompt can include:
1. Role – Who should the AI act as?
2. Task – What should the AI do?
3. Context – What information does the AI need?
4. Requirements – What specific things should be included?
5. Constraints – What should be avoided or limited?
6. Output format – How should the final answer be presented?
7. Audience – Who is the content for?
8. Tone – How should the response sound?
The biggest difference I noticed is that vague prompts leave many things for the AI to assume, while well-structured prompts reduce unnecessary assumptions and produce more focused results.
This is why learning how to write clear prompts is useful when working with AI tools such as ChatGPT and other generative-AI systems. TOPS's current Prompt Engineering curriculum similarly emphasizes prompt basics, prompt patterns, effective use of LLM tools, and hands-on prompt work.

Task 1 – Comparing an Unclear Prompt and a Clear Prompt
Objective
The purpose of this task is to understand the difference between a vague prompt and a clear, specific prompt.
A vague prompt does not provide enough information to the AI, so the response may be too general or may not match what the user actually wants.
A clear prompt gives the AI important information such as:
- What exactly is required
- Time period
- Location
- Category
- Number of results
- Additional information required
- Format of the answer
Prompt 1 – Unclear Prompt
Prompt:
"Give me a list of trending songs in India."

Example AI Response:
Here is a general list of songs that may be popular or trending in India:
1. Aaj Ki Raat
2. Tauba Tauba
3. Akhiyaan Gulaab
4. O Maahi
5. Sajni
6. Teri Baaton Mein Aisa Uljha Jiya
7. Heeriye
8. Apna Bana Le
9. Chaleya
10. Satranga
Problem with this response:
The prompt is not very specific.
For example, it does not tell the AI:
- Which platform should be considered?
- What does "trending" mean?
- Is the user asking for today's songs or this week's songs?
- Should the songs be Bollywood, Punjabi, English, or all languages?
- Should artist names be included?
- How many songs are required?
- What date range should be considered?
Because of this, the AI may give a mixture of old and new songs.

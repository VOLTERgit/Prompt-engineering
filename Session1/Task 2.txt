Task 2 – Common Prompting Mistakes
Objective
People often make mistakes while writing prompts because they assume that the AI already understands their intention.
Below are three common mistakes and their Don't vs. Do examples.
Mistake 1 – Being Too Vague
❌ DON'T
"Order some good food for me on Zomato."

Problem:
The AI does not know:
- What type of food?
- Vegetarian or non-vegetarian?
- Budget?
- How many people?
- Location?
- Spicy or non-spicy?
- Any food preferences?
The word "good" is also subjective.
✅ DO
"Suggest 5 highly rated vegetarian dinner options available on Zomato near my location. My budget is ₹300–₹500 for one person. I prefer Indian food, medium spice, and I want something suitable for dinner. Include the restaurant name, dish name, approximate price, and rating."

Why this is better:
The second prompt provides clear criteria, so the AI can give more relevant suggestions.
Mistake 2 – Not Giving Enough Context
❌ DON'T
"Book a movie ticket for me on BookMyShow."

Problem:
The AI does not know:
- Which movie?
- Which cinema?
- Which city?
- Date?
- Time?
- Number of tickets?
- Seat preference?
✅ DO
"Help me find a movie ticket on BookMyShow. I want to watch a Hindi movie in Ahmedabad this Saturday evening. There will be 2 people. Prefer a show between 6 PM and 9 PM, and choose standard or recliner seats if available. Show me the available options with movie name, theatre, show time, and ticket price."

Why this is better:
The AI now has the important context needed to understand the requirement.
Mistake 3 – Not Specifying the Output Format
❌ DON'T
"Give me some movie recommendations."

Problem:
The response could be a long paragraph, a list, or just movie names.
The user has not explained what information they need.
✅ DO
"Recommend 5 Hindi movies that I can watch this weekend. Present the answer in a table with these columns: Movie Name, Release Year, Genre, IMDb Rating, Main Actors, and a one-line description. Keep each description under 30 words."

Why this is better:
The AI knows exactly how the final answer should look.
Summary of the Three Mistakes

Mistake	                Don't  	                              Do
Too vague	     | "Order good food."   |	Specify cuisine, budget, location, preferences
Lack of context	 |"Book a movie ticket."|	Give city, date, time, people and seat preference
No output format |	"Recommend movies."|	Specify table columns and response length


Conclusion
A good prompt should provide enough information for the AI to understand the goal, context, requirements, and expected output.
